**Read the totals with the sampling in mind.** The checked set is weighted toward Iotova's

checked*, not a scoreline.

## Deception flags

Applied only where the *direction* of a statement is contradicted by a record the speaker
was citing. Eleven flags across 170 dossiers — 9 against Iotova, 2 against Gyurov.

- **Omission (1):** C-03-01 — „signed without a mandate“ is true, but a mandate was
  requested and the speaker refused to table it.
- **Misdirection (10):** e.g. the UN resolution that does not exist, the agreement that
  allegedly lacks the word diplomacy, the gas cutoff attributed to the wrong actor, prices
  described as slowest when they were fastest.

## What the documents settle

- **Kyiv agreement:** ten years, political commitments, outside the Vienna Convention, no
  ratification clause, no troop deployment, Belene only a „potential sale“ of equipment, the
  coalition only „engagement with its activities“. Verified against the 20-page signed text.
- **Budget:** the Constitutional Court struck the labour-code provision that did cut leave
  accrual for part-time workers — so „no rights are cut“ is false and „rights are stolen“ is
  substantially right.
- **NATO drone programme:** both were half-right about different days — refusal documented on
  11 September, cabinet approval on 28 October, accession signed 30 October, the day after
  the debate.
- **Election arithmetic:** „900,000 lost votes“ and „20% poll lead, under 3% actual“ both hold.

## What cannot be settled

- **Botash:** the contract is sealed; every claim resting on its terms is `unverified` by
  policy, including the 13-year term and the €500k/day figure.
- **Personal and anecdotal claims** (32 years of marriage, „never spoke about a revival
  process“, „14 inquiries answered“) — 54 dossiers in total sit at `unverified`, each naming
  what would settle it.

## Source durability

130 evidence items, 35 (27%) with a Wayback snapshot recorded. Most sources are from
September-October 2026 and simply have no archived copy yet; the links are live and the
snapshot field says so rather than implying permanence. Primary documents (the signed
agreement, the Constitutional Court decision) are the strongest anchor and are quoted
verbatim in the dossiers.

## Fairness rules applied

1. Steelmanned every claim before testing it; several verdicts are lenient because of it
   (Gyurov's „every contract needs ratification“ is `mixed`, not `false`, since the
   constitution may impose it independently of the text).
2. No verdict rests on a single secondary outlet where a primary source exists.
3. Where one side's claim rests on a sealed document, the asymmetry is stated rather than
   converted into a verdict.
