# Decision brief — Botash gas agreement

**Decision:** Bulgargaz EAD and BOTAS signed an agreement on 3 January 2023 giving Bulgargaz
access to Turkish LNG regasification terminals and onward transmission, for 13 years
(1 Jan 2023 – 31 Dec 2035), at a reserved-capacity payment of BGN 1 million (EUR 512,000) per
day.

**Decision-maker:** the caretaker government in office on 3 January 2023, under President
Rumen Radev's appointment power. Signed by the two companies' chief executives at the
Council of Ministers.

**Situation at the time (documented):**
- Gazprom Export notified Bulgargaz on 26 April 2022 that supplies would be suspended
  "despite the obligations to the Russian supplier for April 2022 having been fulfilled";
  deliveries stopped at 07:00 on 27 April 2022.
- Bulgaria had no long-term gas supply contract with a non-Russian supplier at that point.
- The agreement is an inter-company commercial contract, classified as a commercial secret,
  never published, and not ratified by parliament.

**Known unknowns (do not fill these by inference):**
- What alternative capacity or supply contracts were on the table in December 2022 –
  January 2023, and at what price.
- Whether the 13-year term was negotiable.
- Who inside the government approved the commercial terms, and on what analysis.
- Whether any legal opinion addressed the ratification question.
