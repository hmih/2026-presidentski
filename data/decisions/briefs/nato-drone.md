# Decision brief — refusal to join the NATO counter-drone programme

**Decision:** on 11 September 2026 the defence minister stated that Bulgaria would not join
NATO's counter-drone initiative, citing an existing similar national capability; at the
14 October 2026 parliamentary hearing he put the second project's mandatory annual
contribution at EUR 220,000 and confirmed Bulgaria had not taken part "from 2025 until now".

**Context at the time:**
- Bulgaria already participates in other air-defence formats, including IRIS-T procurement
  approved by parliament on 15 October 2026, with anti-drone systems to be bought inside the
  IRIS-T contract.
- The Black Sea security environment had deteriorated through 2026: strikes on merchant
  shipping in October, drones crossing Bulgarian territory.
- A counter-drone capability is a stated NATO target.

**Known unknowns:**
- The full cost comparison between joining the NATO programme and the national alternative.
- Whether the ministry had an assessment of the capability gap.
- What the alliance's timeline required.

**Note:** the decision was reversed by the Council of Ministers on 28 October 2026, with the
accession note signed on 30 October 2026. That reversal is *outcome*, not input — do not use
it to score the September decision.
