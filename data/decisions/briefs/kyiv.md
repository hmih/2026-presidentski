# Decision brief — Kyiv security agreement

**Decision:** Bulgaria signed the "Agreement on Cooperation in the Field of Security between
the Republic of Bulgaria and Ukraine" in Kyiv on 30 March 2026, during the caretaker
government of Andrey Gyurov.

**Terms (from the signed 20-page text):** valid ten years from signature; expressly outside
the 1969 Vienna Convention and described as creating "political commitments"; contains no
ratification clause and no requirement that implementing agreements be ratified; refers to a
"potential sale" of equipment acquired for the Belene project; lists UAV and counter-UAV
cooperation among areas of training and joint testing; states that the parties "value the
work of the Coalition of the Willing and confirm their engagement with its activities";
contains no reference to Bulgarian military personnel being deployed to Ukraine.

**Mandate chronology:** on 18 December 2024 caretaker PM Glavchev asked the National Assembly,
through Speaker Kiselova, for a mandate to sign a ten-year security agreement; on
19 December 2024 the speaker declined to table it, holding that signing fell within the
government's competence. The agreement was signed fifteen months later, twenty days before
the 19 April 2026 election.

**Known unknowns:**
- Whether the National Assembly was in session on 30 March 2026.
- What legal advice the caretaker government received on the instrument's status.
- Whether any implementing agreements had been signed by the debate date.
